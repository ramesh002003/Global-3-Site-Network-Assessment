# Risk Rating Guide

Risk ratings are preliminary assessment judgments and should be validated with the client. Consider likelihood, business impact, exposure, existing controls, and compensating measures.

| Rating | Meaning | Example response |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Immediate and severe business, availability, or security exposure verified | Escalate immediately and agree containment/remediation |
| High | Significant risk to critical services or security | Prioritize corrective action |
| Medium | Material weakness or resilience concern | Schedule remediation |
| Low | Limited impact, documentation or optimization issue | Track in improvement backlog |
| Informational | Observation with no confirmed adverse impact | Document for reference |

## Finding quality rules

- Distinguish an observed fact from a potential risk.
- Include evidence and the date/time of observation.
- Explain the affected service or business process.
- Avoid unsupported claims such as “vulnerable,” “non-compliant,” or “single point of failure.”
- Record client acceptance, exception, or remediation decision where applicable.
